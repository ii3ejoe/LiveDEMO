Support Iowa Neighbor

Your Donation Doesn’t Buy Access. It Helps Keep Access Available.

Iowa Neighbor is free to use. Donations are entirely voluntary, and nobody should feel that they need to contribute to use the resources.

So what do donations actually support?

They help cover the practical costs of developing, maintaining, and improving Iowa Neighbor, including:

  • AI subscriptions — tools used to assist with development, research, and maintenance.
  • Supabase — database and backend services when online functionality needs them.
  • Cloudflare — hosting, delivery, and infrastructure that helps keep online features available.

What happens if donations don’t cover the costs? If operating costs eventually exceed available funding, some online features may need to be reduced, limited, or discontinued. The offline foundation is different: functionality already on a user’s device should remain usable even if hosted services are no longer maintained. It may stop receiving updates, but it shouldn’t suddenly require a donation or subscription to keep working.

You can use Iowa Neighbor, share it with others, and benefit from its resources without donating anything. If you have the means and want to help support continued development and online availability, your contribution is appreciated.

Availability before abundance. Quality before quantity. Access before profit.

Use it. Share it. Support it if you can. No guilt, no paywall.

Iowa Neighbor is built from lived experience and a deep understanding of local needs. We’re a free open resource directory—$0 budget, 100% volunteer-led—connecting neighbors with trusted community resources. Every contribution helps us keep the work sustainable and accessible for all.
